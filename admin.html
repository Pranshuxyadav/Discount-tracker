Discount watch

Same-day discount per product on Supertails, Amazon and Flipkart, measured against our own MRP
High discount means above %

Discount = (our MRP − selling price) ÷ our MRP. Treat MRPs are per carton in the May list, so they are divided by 15. Above the threshold means the platform is running a high discount. It’s a signal, not a limit. A price is marked suspect, and left out of every number, when its listed MRP is 0 or it is far below our MRP. Where a platform has two listings for one EAN, the lower valid price is used.
